The service

Metanoia Labs provides career-preparation tools: a free readiness assessment (the Career Map), learning modules organised in five pillars, and a progress tracker (The Compass). We are in early access: some parts are live, some are in development, and every product states which on its own page.

What we do not promise: Metanoia does not guarantee employment, interviews, offers, scholarships, or admission to any programme. We build your readiness; hiring decisions belong to employers. Anyone in this market who promises you a job is selling you something else.

02

Eligibility and your account

You must be at least 17 to use Metanoia. Keep your credentials to yourself, tell us if your account is compromised, and give us information that is true. Student pricing requires verification of active student status.

03

Subscriptions, billing, and cancellation

  • The free tier is free, always, and requires no card.
  • Paid plans renew on the schedule shown at purchase. We state the renewal price before you commit, and we email you before any renewal.

Refund policy: 14 days, no questions asked. If a paid plan is not what you hoped, tell us within 14 days of purchase and we refund it in full.
