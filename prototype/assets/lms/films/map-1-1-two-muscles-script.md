# Two muscles: adaptability and resilience

Film `map-1-1-two-muscles` · The Map 1.1

**Source note.** Written for this lesson from its own objectives and its recovery loop (Name, Normalise, Extract, Next step). The story and the character are invented. No third-party script, footage, picture or sound.

## English (135 s)

- `00:04` Tuesday, 7:40. Laras opens her inbox before work.
- `00:09` Rejection number nine. This time she failed an online test at stage two.
- `00:15` An hour later, her internship team stops using the tool she learned last month.
- `00:22` Two bad surprises in one morning. But they are not the same problem.
- `00:28` The new tool asks Laras to change her method. That is adaptability.
- `00:35` The rejection drains her energy. Getting it back is resilience.
- `00:40` You can be strong in one and weak in the other. And both can be trained.
- `00:48` For the rejection, Laras runs a recovery loop. It has four steps and takes fifteen minutes.
- `00:55` Name it, precisely. Not “I am bad at this”, but “I failed the numerical test at stage two.”
- `01:04` Normalise it. Most applicants are rejected most of the time. This is the terrain, not a verdict on her.
- `01:13` Extract one thing she controls. She ran out of time on the last ten questions.
- `01:20` Choose the next step, and put it in the calendar. Twenty minutes of timed practice, tomorrow at eight.
- `01:28` For the new tool, the answer is different. She changes her method.
- `01:35` She asks a teammate for fifteen minutes, then rebuilds one small report by herself.
- `01:41` By Friday, nobody calls it the new tool any more. It is just the tool.
- `01:49` A setback you process becomes data.
- `01:52` A setback you avoid becomes fear.
- `01:56` Adaptability is a decision about method.
- `02:00` Resilience is a discipline about energy.
- `02:04` Now it is your turn. Pick one setback from the last month, and run the loop.
