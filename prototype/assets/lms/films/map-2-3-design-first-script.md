# Design first: a career by design, not by first offer

Film `map-2-3-design-first` · The Map 2.3

**Source note.** Written for this lesson from its own objectives and reading: a mission that names contribution instead of titles, a three-year outcome with four parts, and the default scripts that run a career nobody designed. The ideas are long-established and taught here in the lesson's own words. The story and the character are invented. No third-party script, footage, picture or sound.

## English (120 s)

- `00:04` Friday. Yoga opens his inbox and finds a job offer. His first.
- `00:10` He is about to accept. Not because it fits, but because it came first.
- `00:17` One question stops him. Whose script is this line from?
- `00:22` Without a design, three scripts compete to run a career. The inheritance script: what the family respects.
- `00:30` The feed script: whatever is trending this year.
- `00:35` And the first-offer script: whoever says yes first.
- `00:39` None of them is malicious. They are just unauthored.
- `00:44` So he designs first. His first mission sentence names a title.
- `00:50` A title is not a direction.
- `00:54` The rewrite names a contribution: I help small businesses understand their own numbers.
- `01:00` A mission is a compass. It needs a first landmark: one outcome, three years out, with four parts.
- `01:09` A domain.
- `01:12` A level.
- `01:15` Evidence someone else can see.
- `01:18` And a line you refuse to cross.
- `01:22` On Monday he answers the offer with two questions. Will I work with small clients? Who will I learn from?
- `01:31` He may still say yes. But now the yes is his: direction by design, not by first offer.
- `01:40` A mission names contribution, not titles.
- `01:43` A 3-year outcome makes it plannable.
- `01:47` Now it is your turn. For one big choice you face, ask: whose script is this line from?
