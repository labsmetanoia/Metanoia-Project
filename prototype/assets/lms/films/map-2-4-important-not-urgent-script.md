# Important, not urgent: defending the work that builds a career

Film `map-2-4-important-not-urgent` · The Map 2.4

**Source note.** Written for this lesson from its own objectives and reading: sorting a week by urgent and important, a twenty-minute weekly preview, and a graceful no with three parts. The ideas are long-established and taught here in the lesson's own words. The story and the character are invented. No third-party script, footage, picture or sound.

## English (131 s)

- `00:04` Friday, six in the evening. Rani has been busy all week, and nothing that matters has moved.
- `00:12` She answered every ping and joined every meeting. Her certification practice got zero hours.
- `00:19` Urgency is a sensation, not a measure of value. Important work never taps you on the shoulder.
- `00:27` Sort any week into four boxes. First: urgent and important. Real deadlines, real crises.
- `00:34` Second: important, not urgent. Practice, planning, people, health. Careers are built here.
- `00:40` Third: urgent, not important. Most pings and many meetings.
- `00:45` Fourth: neither.
- `00:48` The trap: box three dresses up as box one, and eats the hours box two needed.
- `00:56` The defence is a weekly preview. Twenty minutes, three moves. Review where you are going.
- `01:03` Choose two or three important blocks, and put them in the calendar first.
- `01:10` Then preview the known deadlines, so they get space instead of an all-nighter.
- `01:16` A calendar block needs one more defence: a rehearsed no. Not a fake maybe.
- `01:23` A fake maybe becomes a yes delivered late and badly.
- `01:28` A graceful no has three parts: thanks, the reason you are protecting, and an alternative.
- `01:36` The next Thursday, seven in the evening. The pings still arrive. The block holds.
- `01:42` Every yes is an unpriced no to something else. Price it before you give it.
- `01:50` Important work never taps you. Schedule it first.
- `01:54` Defend it with a calendar block and a rehearsed no.
- `02:00` Now it is your turn. Book twenty minutes this weekend for your first weekly preview.
