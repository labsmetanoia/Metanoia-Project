# Answer first: designing a message from the reader backwards

Film `map-4-2-answer-first` · The Map 4.2

**Source note.** Written for this lesson from its own objectives and reading: designing from the reader's decision backwards, the order of answer, reasons and evidence, and moving between summary and detail on request. These are standard practices of workplace communication, taught here in the lesson's own words. The case and the character are invented for this film and appear in no other course. No third-party script, footage, picture or sound.

## English (114 s)

- `00:04` Nisa has a week of analysis on late deliveries, and a manager with ninety seconds between two meetings.
- `00:13` So she does not start from her analysis. She designs the message backwards, from the reader.
- `00:20` Who is this for? A manager with ninety seconds.
- `00:25` What must she decide? Whether to switch courier.
- `00:30` What does she already know? That deliveries are late. So Nisa skips it.
- `00:36` Professional structure turns the school essay upside down. Line one is the answer: switch courier for the East Java routes.
- `00:45` Under it, the two or three reasons that support it.
- `00:50` And under each reason, the evidence, for readers who want it.
- `00:56` Answer first is a courtesy and a filter. Busy readers can stop after line one.
- `01:03` Skilled communicators fly at three altitudes, and say when they change.
- `01:09` High up: one sentence.
- `01:12` Lower: the structure.
- `01:15` On the ground: the numbers.
- `01:18` Her manager interrupts: which routes exactly? That is not an attack. It is a request to go lower, so Nisa goes to the ground.
- `01:27` Then she closes the loop with the cheapest quality check there is: did that answer it?
- `01:35` Design from the reader's decision backwards.
- `01:39` Answer, reasons, evidence. In that order.
- `01:43` Now it is your turn. Rewrite your next update so that line one is the answer.

## Bahasa Indonesia (108 s)
