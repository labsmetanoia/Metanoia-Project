# Diagnose first: root causes and action plans

Film `map-3-2-diagnose-first` · The Map 3.2

**Source note.** Written for this lesson from its own objectives: a hypothesis written with a number, reading where people drop off, sorting ideas by impact and ease, and a plan with owners and dates. The methods are standard problem-solving practice, taught here in the lesson's own words. The case, the numbers and the character are invented for this film and appear in no other course or book. No third-party script, footage, picture or sound.

## English (128 s)

- `00:04` Arga sells iced coffee from a cart near campus. Sales are down, and he already has a cure: a discount.
- `00:13` His friend asks one question first. What is the diagnosis? A cure for the wrong cause is not a cure.
- `00:22` He starts with a feeling: people think my coffee is too expensive.
- `00:28` A feeling cannot be tested.
- `00:31` So he writes a hypothesis with a number in it. About six in ten buyers never return, because of the price.
- `00:40` Before changing anything, he counts for one week. Twelve hundred people walk past the cart.
- `00:48` Only three hundred notice it.
- `00:51` One hundred and eighty buy, and one hundred and twenty of them come back.
- `00:58` His guess was wrong. Two in three buyers return. The leak is earlier: most people never notice the cart.
- `01:07` Now he lists ideas, and sorts them by impact and ease. A tall sign and a brighter canopy: high impact, easy. First.
- `01:16` Moving to the main gate would help more, but it is hard. Asking for help can make a hard idea easier.
- `01:25` The rest can wait.
- `01:28` Then every action gets an owner and a date. The sign, by Saturday.
- `01:34` The canopy, by Monday.
- `01:37` And in two weeks he counts again. A plan without owners and dates is only a wish.
- `01:45` Symptom, diagnosis, prescription. In that order.
- `01:49` Write your guess with a number, so data can kill it.
- `01:55` Now it is your turn. Pick one problem you are about to fix. Write your hypothesis with a number before you act.

## Bahasa Indonesia (114 s)

- `00:04` Arga berjualan es kopi dengan gerobak di dekat kampus. Penjualannya turun, dan ia sudah punya obatnya: diskon.
- `00:12` Temannya bertanya dulu. Apa diagnosisnya? Obat untuk penyebab yang salah bukanlah obat.
- `00:18` Ia berangkat dari perasaan: orang merasa kopiku kemahalan.
- `00:23` Perasaan tidak bisa diuji.
- `00:26` Maka ia menulis hipotesis yang memuat angka. Sekitar enam dari sepuluh pembeli tidak kembali karena harganya.
- `00:33` Sebelum mengubah apa pun, ia menghitung selama seminggu. Seribu dua ratus orang lewat di depan gerobaknya.
- `00:41` Hanya tiga ratus yang melihatnya.
- `00:44` Seratus delapan puluh membeli, dan seratus dua puluh di antaranya kembali lagi.
- `00:51` Tebakannya keliru. Dua dari tiga pembeli kembali. Bocornya lebih awal: kebanyakan orang tidak pernah melihat gerobaknya.
- `00:58` Kini ia mendaftar ide, lalu memilahnya menurut dampak dan kemudahan. Papan tinggi dan tenda yang lebih terang: dampak besar, mudah. Dikerjakan dulu.
- `01:07` Pindah ke gerbang utama lebih besar dampaknya, tetapi sulit. Meminta bantuan bisa membuat ide yang sulit menjadi lebih mudah.
- `01:16` Sisanya bisa menunggu.
- `01:19` Lalu setiap tindakan diberi pemilik dan tanggal. Papan, paling lambat Sabtu.
- `01:25` Tenda, paling lambat Senin.
- `01:28` Dan dua pekan lagi ia menghitung ulang. Rencana tanpa pemilik dan tanggal hanyalah angan-angan.
- `01:35` Gejala, diagnosis, resep. Dengan urutan itu.
- `01:38` Tulis tebakanmu dengan angka, supaya data bisa mematahkannya.
- `01:43` Sekarang giliranmu. Pilih satu masalah yang hendak kamu perbaiki. Tulis hipotesismu dengan angka sebelum bertindak.
