## English (75 s)

- `00:00` Diagnose first: root causes and action plans.
- `00:03` Arga sells iced coffee from a cart near campus. Sales are down, and he already has a cure: a discount.
- `00:09` His friend asks one question first. What is the diagnosis? A cure for the wrong cause is not a cure.
- `00:16` He starts with a feeling: people think my coffee is too expensive. A feeling cannot be tested. So he writes a hypothesis with a number in it.
- `00:24` Before changing anything, he counts for one week. Twelve hundred people walk past the cart. Only three hundred notice it. One hundred and eighty buy, and one hundred and twenty of them come back.
- `00:33` His guess was wrong. Two in three buyers return.
- `00:40` The leak is earlier: most people never notice the cart.
- `00:45` Now he lists ideas, and sorts them by impact and ease. A tall sign and a brighter canopy: high impact, easy. First. Moving to the main gate would help more, but it is hard.
- `00:54` Then every action gets an owner and a date. The sign, by Saturday. The canopy, by Monday. And in two weeks he counts again. A plan without owners and dates is only a wish.
- `01:02` Symptom, diagnosis, prescription. In that order. Write your guess with a number, so data can kill it.
- `01:10` Now it is your turn. Pick one problem you are about to fix. Write your hypothesis with a number before you act.
