# Three hard messages: a cold email, an interruption, a disagreement

Film `map-4-3-three-hard-messages` · The Map 4.3

**Source note.** Written for this lesson from its own objectives and reading: a cold email whose first line earns the second, an update that survives an interruption, and disagreeing upward with data, an option and a question. These are standard practices of workplace communication, taught here in the lesson's own words. The case and the character are invented for this film and appear in no other course. No third-party script, footage, picture or sound.

## English (111 s)

- `00:04` First, the cold email. Bagas writes to an alumna he has never met. His first draft talks about himself, and asks for sometime.
- `00:13` The second draft shows his homework in line one. And the ask is small, specific and time-boxed.
- `00:21` The first line has one job: to earn the second line.
- `00:27` Second, the interruption. Bagas is thirty seconds into his update when his lead cuts in: wait, what is the bottom line?
- `00:36` It is not an attack. It is a request for a different altitude. He answers in one sentence, then asks: shall I go into the detail?
- `00:45` Third, disagreeing upward. His lead wants to spend the whole budget on one channel. Bagas thinks: that plan is wrong.
- `00:54` Said aloud, that sentence defends his ego, not the decision.
- `00:59` So he shares the data, names an option, and asks a real question: what am I missing?
- `01:07` Put the three side by side. A cold email works when the ask is small, specific and time-boxed.
- `01:16` An interruption is navigation, not attack.
- `01:19` A disagreement lands with data, an option and a question.
- `01:25` Each one starts from the other person, not from you.
- `01:30` The first line must earn the second.
- `01:34` Disagree to protect the decision, not your ego.
- `01:39` Now it is your turn. Draft your own version of each message before you open the debriefs below.

## Bahasa Indonesia (100 s)

- `00:04` Pertama, email perkenalan. Bagas menulis kepada seorang alumni yang belum pernah ia temui. Draf pertamanya bercerita tentang dirinya, dan meminta waktu kapan-kapan.
- `00:13` Draf kedua menunjukkan sejak baris pertama bahwa ia sudah mencari tahu. Permintaannya kecil, spesifik, dan berbatas waktu.
- `00:21` Baris pertama punya satu tugas: membuat baris kedua dibaca.
- `00:26` Kedua, interupsi. Baru tiga puluh detik Bagas memaparkan laporannya, atasannya memotong: sebentar, intinya apa?
- `00:33` Itu bukan serangan. Itu permintaan untuk pindah ketinggian. Ia menjawab dalam satu kalimat, lalu bertanya: perlu saya jelaskan rinciannya?
- `00:42` Ketiga, tidak setuju kepada atasan. Atasannya ingin menghabiskan seluruh anggaran di satu kanal. Bagas membatin: rencana itu salah.
- `00:50` Jika diucapkan, kalimat itu membela egonya, bukan keputusannya.
- `00:55` Maka ia membagikan datanya, menawarkan satu pilihan, dan mengajukan pertanyaan yang sungguh-sungguh: apa yang saya lewatkan?
- `01:03` Sandingkan ketiganya. Email perkenalan berhasil jika permintaannya kecil, spesifik, dan berbatas waktu.
- `01:09` Interupsi adalah navigasi, bukan serangan.
- `01:12` Ketidaksetujuan diterima jika membawa data, pilihan, dan pertanyaan.
- `01:16` Semuanya berangkat dari lawan bicaramu, bukan dari dirimu.
- `01:21` Baris pertama harus membuat baris kedua dibaca.
- `01:25` Tidak setujulah demi keputusannya, bukan demi egomu.
- `01:29` Sekarang giliranmu. Tulis versimu sendiri untuk tiap pesan sebelum membuka pembahasan di bawah.
