# Four small moves: how a strong relationship is built

Film `map-5-3-four-small-moves` · The Map 5.3

**Source note.** Written for this lesson from its own objectives and reading: why relationships carry learning and resilience, attention, vulnerability, empathy and compassion inside one everyday conversation, psychological safety from a junior position, and a boundary in three parts. The four qualities are ordinary words and are taught here without any course's acronym; the film names John Gottman once for the term bid for connection. The case and the characters are invented for this film and appear in no other course. No third-party script, footage, picture or sound.

## English (169 s)

- `00:04` Salma is three months into her first job. At the next desk, Yudha puts down the phone and sighs.
- `00:13` A sigh like that is a small request for attention. The psychologist John Gottman calls it a bid for connection.
- `00:22` She can turn toward it, miss it, or brush it off. Trust is built in these seconds, many times a day.
- `00:31` She turns toward it. Phone face down, chair turned, and she lets him finish. That is attention, and the other three are impossible without it.
- `00:40` Then she says something true about herself: I froze on my first call too. That is vulnerability. It keeps the conversation honest.
- `00:49` Before any advice, she says what she heard: sounds like it hit harder than you expected. That is empathy.
- `00:58` And she closes with an act, not a sentiment: shall we rehearse the next one at four? That is compassion.
- `01:07` Everyone agrees with the four words.
- `01:11` Few practise them in an ordinary week.
- `01:15` So pick one person and one move.
- `01:19` A team learns fastest when people can speak up and admit mistakes. That is psychological safety, and everyone's behaviour builds it.
- `01:28` From the junior seat it starts with asking: I don't follow yet, could you show me?
- `01:35` Owning a mistake early: I got the file wrong, and here is the fix.
- `01:42` And thanking the person who corrected you.
- `01:46` Small, consistent acts from juniors change a team faster than they expect.
- `01:53` On Tuesday her supervisor adds a second task to a full week. Saying no feels rude, so Salma almost says yes to both.
- `02:02` But a yes she cannot keep will hurt the relationship more than an honest limit.
- `02:09` So she states a boundary in three parts. A commitment: I want to do this well.
- `02:17` A limit: the report by Thursday, but not the slides too. And an option: could the slides wait until Monday?
- `02:26` Trust is built in seconds, with one person at a time.
- `02:31` A boundary stated early and kindly protects the relationship.
- `02:36` Now it is your turn. Name one person, and choose one of the four moves to practise with them this week.

## Bahasa Indonesia (159 s)

- `00:04` Salma baru tiga bulan di pekerjaan pertamanya. Di meja sebelah, Yudha meletakkan telepon dan menghela napas.
- `00:12` Helaan napas seperti itu adalah permintaan kecil untuk diperhatikan. Psikolog John Gottman menyebutnya ajakan untuk terhubung.
- `00:19` Ia bisa menghadap, berpaling, atau menepisnya. Kepercayaan dibangun dalam detik-detik seperti ini, berkali-kali dalam sehari.
- `00:27` Ia menghadap. Ponsel ditelungkupkan, kursi diputar, dan ia membiarkan Yudha selesai bicara. Itulah perhatian, dan tiga langkah lainnya mustahil tanpanya.
- `00:36` Lalu ia mengatakan sesuatu yang jujur tentang dirinya: saya juga membeku di telepon pertama saya. Itulah keterbukaan. Ia menjaga percakapan tetap jujur.
- `00:45` Sebelum memberi saran apa pun, ia menyebutkan apa yang ia dengar: sepertinya itu lebih berat dari yang Mas kira. Itulah empati.
- `00:54` Dan ia menutup dengan tindakan, bukan sekadar simpati: mau latihan untuk telepon berikutnya jam empat? Itulah welas asih.
- `01:02` Semua orang setuju dengan empat kata itu.
- `01:06` Sedikit yang mempraktikkannya di minggu biasa.
- `01:10` Maka pilih satu orang dan satu langkah.
- `01:14` Tim belajar paling cepat ketika orang berani bersuara dan mengakui kesalahan. Itulah rasa aman psikologis, dan perilaku semua orang ikut membangunnya.
- `01:23` Dari kursi junior, ia dimulai dengan bertanya: saya belum paham, boleh ditunjukkan?
- `01:29` Mengakui kesalahan lebih awal: saya keliru di berkas itu, dan ini perbaikannya.
- `01:35` Dan berterima kasih kepada orang yang mengoreksimu.
- `01:39` Tindakan kecil yang konsisten dari para junior mengubah tim lebih cepat dari yang mereka kira.
- `01:47` Hari Selasa, atasannya menambah tugas kedua di minggu yang sudah penuh. Salma merasa sungkan, sehingga ia hampir mengiyakan keduanya.
- `01:56` Padahal, kesanggupan yang tak bisa ia tepati akan lebih merusak relasi daripada batas yang jujur.
- `02:03` Maka ia menyampaikan batasan dalam tiga bagian. Komitmen: saya ingin mengerjakannya dengan baik.
- `02:09` Batas: laporannya selesai Kamis, tapi tidak sekaligus dengan slidenya. Dan pilihan: bolehkah slidenya menunggu sampai Senin?
- `02:17` Kepercayaan dibangun dalam hitungan detik, satu orang demi satu orang.
- `02:22` Batasan yang disampaikan lebih awal dengan baik melindungi relasi.
- `02:27` Sekarang giliranmu. Sebut satu orang, lalu pilih satu dari empat langkah untuk kamu praktikkan dengannya minggu ini.
