# A goal you can count: gaps and good decisions

Film `map-3-3-a-goal-you-can-count` · The Map 3.3

**Source note.** Written for this lesson from its own objectives: a solid goal with what, by when and conditions, the gap against doing nothing, a pyramid of reasons checked with data, options weighed against criteria, and challenging your own conclusion. The methods are standard problem-solving practice, taught here in the lesson's own words. The case, the numbers and the character are invented for this film and appear in no other course or book. No third-party script, footage, picture or sound.

## English (147 s)

- `00:04` Dewi wants a scholarship abroad. On her wall there is one line: improve my English.
- `00:11` That is a wish. You cannot calculate a gap for a wish.
- `00:17` A solid goal has three parts. What: a language test score of 6.5.
- `00:24` By when: the first of March.
- `00:28` And under which conditions: without leaving her part-time job.
- `00:33` Now the gap is a number. Today she scores 5.5. At her current pace she reaches 5.9. The gap is 0.6.
- `00:42` She stacks her plan as a pyramid. On top, the conclusion: I can close the gap by the first of March.
- `00:51` Underneath, the reasons that must add up to it: writing, listening, speaking.
- `00:57` Then she checks each reason with data: two timed tests.
- `01:02` Writing and listening hold. Speaking does not improve when she practises alone.
- `01:08` If the reasons do not add up, there is no plan yet. She needs a speaking partner.
- `01:16` For speaking she has two options. A plain list of pros and cons would favour the cheaper course.
- `01:25` So she lists criteria that come from the goal, and gives them weights. Speaking practice weighs most.
- `01:33` Fitting her work hours comes second, because that was her condition.
- `01:38` Cost comes third.
- `01:41` Weighted, the online tutor wins: sixteen to eleven.
- `01:46` Before she commits, she challenges her own conclusion. She asks a senior who won the same scholarship.
- `01:54` One conversation gives her a fact that a week of worrying never did: the interview matters as much as the score.
- `02:03` A goal without a gap you can count is a wish.
- `02:09` Criteria come from the goal. Weights come from the criteria.
- `02:14` Now it is your turn. Write one goal with what, by when and conditions. Then calculate the gap.

## Bahasa Indonesia (134 s)

- `00:04` Dewi mengincar beasiswa ke luar negeri. Di dindingnya ada satu kalimat: memperbaiki bahasa Inggrisku.
- `00:11` Itu keinginan. Kesenjangan dari sebuah keinginan tidak bisa dihitung.
- `00:16` Tujuan yang kokoh punya tiga bagian. Apa: skor tes bahasa 6,5.
- `00:22` Kapan: sebelum 1 Maret.
- `00:25` Dan dengan syarat apa: tanpa berhenti dari kerja paruh waktunya.
- `00:30` Kini kesenjangannya berupa angka. Hari ini skornya 5,5. Dengan laju sekarang ia sampai di 5,9. Kesenjangannya 0,6.
- `00:38` Ia menyusun rencananya sebagai piramida. Di puncak, kesimpulannya: aku bisa menutup kesenjangan itu sebelum 1 Maret.
- `00:46` Di bawahnya, alasan-alasan yang harus menjumlah ke kesimpulan itu: menulis, menyimak, berbicara.
- `00:52` Lalu ia mengecek setiap alasan dengan data: dua tes berwaktu.
- `00:57` Menulis dan menyimak bertahan. Berbicara tidak membaik jika ia berlatih sendirian.
- `01:03` Jika alasan-alasannya tidak menjumlah, itu belum menjadi rencana. Ia butuh rekan berlatih berbicara.
- `01:09` Untuk berbicara ia punya dua opsi. Daftar untung-rugi biasa akan memenangkan kursus yang lebih murah.
- `01:17` Maka ia mendaftar kriteria yang lahir dari tujuannya, lalu memberi bobot. Latihan berbicara paling berat bobotnya.
- `01:24` Kecocokan dengan jam kerja ada di urutan kedua, karena itulah syaratnya.
- `01:30` Biaya ada di urutan ketiga.
- `01:33` Setelah diberi bobot, tutor daring unggul: enam belas lawan sebelas.
- `01:39` Sebelum memutuskan, ia menantang kesimpulannya sendiri. Ia bertanya kepada kakak tingkat yang pernah meraih beasiswa yang sama.
- `01:47` Satu percakapan memberinya fakta yang tak pernah ia dapat dari seminggu mencemaskan: wawancara sama pentingnya dengan skor.
- `01:55` Tujuan tanpa kesenjangan yang bisa dihitung hanyalah keinginan.
- `01:59` Kriteria lahir dari tujuan. Bobot lahir dari kriteria.
- `02:04` Sekarang giliranmu. Tulis satu tujuan dengan apa, kapan, dan syaratnya. Lalu hitung kesenjangannya.
