- `00:00` From complaint to problem: the four-step method.
- `00:03` Week nine. Kirana runs her faculty's peer tutoring, and the room is nearly empty. Her team says: nobody comes any more.
- `00:09` That is a complaint. It is not yet a problem she can work on.
- `00:16` Problem solving is a loop with four steps, walked in order. Understand the problem. Find the root cause. Make a plan. Execute, and adjust.
- `00:24` Most people skip a step, and always the same one. Some stop at the complaint. Some judge every plan and never make one. Some have ideas all day, and no dates. And some act at once, without a diagnosis.

- `00:54` And the obstacle: since midterms, the sessions clash with the new lab schedule. Now it is a problem someone can work on.
- `01:00` A complaint becomes a problem when it has a number and an obstacle. Notice which step you skip.
