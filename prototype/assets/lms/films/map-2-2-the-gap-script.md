## English (74 s)

- `00:00` The gap: acting inside your control.
- `00:03` Ten at night. A message from a teammate: I have not started my part. The presentation is tomorrow.
- `00:09` Sinta's thumbs are already typing something sharp.
- `00:15` But between the message and her reply there is a gap. It is about two seconds wide.
- `00:21` The stimulus is fixed. She did not choose it. The gap is where she chooses. And the response is entirely hers. Ownership is refusing to let the stimulus write your reply.

- `00:38` Your language is a live diagnostic. I have to, they made me, there is nothing I can do: these are reactive tells.

- `01:01` The stimulus is fixed. The response is yours. Your words show who holds the pen.
- `01:09` Now it is your turn. Pick one frustrating moment from this week. Find the gap, and rewrite your reply.
