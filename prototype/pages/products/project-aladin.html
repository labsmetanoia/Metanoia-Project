Course Description

Project Aladdin is the starting point of the Metanoia career system. Before you can plan the right career, interview for the right role, or build the right professional profile, you need to understand who you are — what drives you, where your strengths lie, and what kind of professional you intend to become.

This course guides you through six structured learning modules covering self-awareness, the habits of effective people, structured problem-solving, communication for impact, well-being and digital capability, and virtual career preparation. By the time you complete Project Aladdin, you won't just have a clearer picture of yourself — you'll have a career direction grounded in genuine self-knowledge rather than guesswork.

Skills Covered in This Course

Self-Awareness Adaptability Critical Thinking Communication Problem-Solving Career Readiness Digital Productivity Professional Habits

What You'll Learn

Understand your strengths, values, and professional identity

Communicate with clarity and impact in professional settings

Module 01 · The Map Available

Personal Audit — Strengths, Patterns, and Goals Reflection

Assignment Brief

Reflect on your core strengths, your behavioral patterns under pressure, and your career goals for the next two years. This assignment forms the foundation of your Metanoia Career Profile — the personal map that everything else in your career system is built on top of.

Format: Written reflection (500–700 words) 100 pts Self-paced
Module 02 · Habits Locked — complete Module 2 first

My Habits — A 30-Day Personal Application Plan

Assignment Brief

Identify one core principle from each of the three habits covered in this module and create a concrete 30-day action plan for applying them in your academic or professional life. The plan should include specific, observable daily actions and a simple reflection format for tracking follow-through.

Format: Written plan + daily commitment tracker 100 pts Self-paced
Module 03 · Problem Solving Locked — complete Module 3 first

Problem Statement and Solution Brief

Assignment Brief

Select a real challenge you are currently facing — academic, personal, or professional — and apply the structured problem-solving framework from this module. Define the problem precisely using the provided template, generate at least three distinct solutions, evaluate each against your criteria, and select and clearly justify your recommended approach.

Format: Structured brief (400–600 words) following the module framework template 100 pts Self-paced
Module 04 · Communication Locked — complete Module 4 first

Professional Introduction — Video Assessment

Assignment Brief

Record a 3-minute video introducing yourself for a professional context — an internship interview, a company presentation, or a networking event. Your introduction should demonstrate the communication principles covered in this module: clarity, structure, energy, and professional presence. After recording, self-assess your performance using the communication rubric provided in the module before submitting.

Format: Video submission (3 minutes) + self-assessment form 100 pts Self-paced
Module 05 · Well-Being & Digital Locked — complete Module 5 first

My Digital Productivity System

Assignment Brief

Design and document a personal digital productivity system using at least three tools introduced in this module. Submit a one-page system overview that explains: what your system is designed to achieve, which tools you chose and why, how the tools work together within your workflow, and how you plan to maintain and review the system over the next 90 days.

Format: One-page system design with tool screenshots 100 pts Self-paced
Module 06 · Career Simulation Locked — complete Module 6 first

Virtual Job Simulation — Reflection Report

Assignment Brief

Complete one full virtual job simulation from your chosen industry track and write a structured reflection report. Your report should cover four sections: (1) what the simulation required and how it was structured; (2) how you performed against the stated evaluation criteria; (3) what you learned about the industry, the role, and your own professional readiness; and (4) what you would approach differently in a real application process.

Choose your industry track:
Format: Structured reflection (600–800 words) following the provided template 100 pts Self-paced
