About Us

Who is building this, why, where it stands, how it will make money, and what we have committed to.

01 · The origin

I did not start Metanoia because I had a product idea. I started it because of a pattern I could not stop seeing, and could not explain away.

Capable people, with the degree and the work ethic they were told would be enough, sending out application after application and hearing nothing back. Then concluding, quietly, that the problem was them. It was not them.

What they were missing was never talent. It was the things nobody teaches: how screening actually works, what an interviewer is really scoring, which door to try first, what a fair offer looks like, and someone to ask before the interview that counts. The people who had those things usually had them from a family, a senior alumnus, or a consultant they could afford. Everyone else was navigating without the map.

That is the whole of it. The career operating system exists. It is just not distributed fairly. So I began writing it down — first as documents, then as a prototype, now as the platform you are looking at — for the people it was never handed to.

— The founder, Metanoia Labs

02 · What we believe

The career operating system already exists. Insider knowledge of how hiring works, practice before the attempts that count, honest feedback, warm introductions, and the confidence that comes from all of that — it is real, and it works.

It is distributed through well-connected families, senior alumni and expensive consultants. Opportunity ends up being a function of access, not capability.

We are building it for everyone else. Not a cheaper version of a coaching service, but the infrastructure itself: written down, honest about what it knows and does not know, connected to real employers, and free to start.

We also believe the platform only works if people can trust it, which is why every claim on it should trace to a source, every score should come with its reasons, and nobody's individual data should ever be sold to an employer.

03 · The team

Right now, Metanoia is one person and a growing group of collaborators.

The founder designs and builds the platform, writes the Mind Palace with editorial reviewers, and does the mentoring by hand while the software catches up. Collaborators contribute reviews of hiring processes they have been through, editorial checks on the Indonesian copy, and the occasional argument about what a page should say.

04 · Where we are

Stage: beta. What you are using is a working prototype of the platform. The courses, the company directory, the assessment and the Mind Palace are real and public; accounts, payments and mentor matching are not built yet, and anything you save is stored in your own browser rather than on a server.

05 · How we make money

Indonesia's career-services market has earned a certain suspicion, so here is the answer to the question you are probably asking.

  • Beta is free. Basecamp — the Career Map, your readiness score, the Gauntlet guide, your route outline and the community — is free, always. Everything beyond it is Rp 0 while we build.
  • Paid editions come at commercial launch: Student, Fresh Graduate, Early Professional and Mature Professional memberships, priced to sit far below a bootcamp and visibly above free.
  • We will tell you before anything changes. Nothing you use for free today will start charging you without notice, and early-access pricing is locked for as long as you stay subscribed.
  • We take no commission from employers for placing you. Enterprise programmes pay for readiness programmes and aggregate, anonymised reporting — never for access to you.

The full breakdown, with what is live today and what is not, is on the pricing page.
