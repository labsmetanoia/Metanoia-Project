Negotiation Script — Nexus Module 8

Step 1 — Gratitude first, numbers second. Never negotiate in the same breath as the offer. Thank them, confirm your enthusiasm, and ask for 2–3 days to review.

“Thank you — I'm genuinely excited about this offer. I'd like to take two days to review the full package carefully. Can we speak again on Thursday?”

Step 2 — Anchor on value, not need. Reference the scope of the role and your evidence — never your expenses. State your target as a researched range, top-anchored.

“Based on the scope we discussed and market data for this role, I was expecting something in the range of X to Y. Is there flexibility to close that gap?”

Step 3 — Negotiate the package, not just the salary. If base is fixed, move to signing bonus, review timeline, allowances, or start date. Every element is negotiable once.

Step 4 — Get it in writing. Whatever is agreed verbally, confirm by email the same day. The full framework lives in The Rope Module 8.
