Course Description

The Rope is the performance system of the Metanoia career trilogy — The Rope. After mapping your direction (The Map) and building your application arsenal (The Pack), you now face the most exposed pitch of the climb: the human interview.

Most candidates who reach the interview stage still lose — not from lack of competence, but from lack of system. They have no structured story library. They cannot decode the competency framework beneath the questions. They do not know how to perform differently across HR, Technical, User, and Final interview formats. And when the offer comes, they accept the first number they hear.

The Rope changes that. Nine structured modules take you from understanding the psychology of hiring judgment through building your 30-story STAR library, mastering competency frameworks, performing across all interview types, running AI-powered and peer simulation drills — and finally, evaluating and negotiating your offer, then succeeding in your first 90 days.

The rope does not guarantee you will never slip. It guarantees that when you do, it holds — and you complete the climb.

Practice Assignments

9 assignments · 900 total points · Submit any time · Feedback within 3 business days

9Assignments900Points100Pts Each
Module 01 · Inside the Room Available

Interview Format & Decision Psychology Map

Assignment Brief

Choose one target role from your career direction (from The Map). Research and map the complete hiring process for that role at a specific company: stages, interview formats expected, approximate timeline, and decision-makers at each stage. Write a one-page "battlefield brief" you would use to prepare yourself and a peer who is applying for the same role.

Format: Interview map + 300-word interviewer brief 100 pts Self-paced
Module 02 · Story Library Locked — complete Module 2 first

Story Library Draft (10 Stories)

Assignment Brief

Using the STAR-L framework from Module 2, write 10 structured competency stories drawn from your own experience — at least one per competency category from the first 10 categories listed in Lesson 2.2. Each story: 200–300 words. Submit as a structured document with clear competency labels.

Format: Structured document: 10 STAR-L stories (200–300 words each) 100 pts Self-paced
Module 03 · Competency Intelligence Locked — complete Module 3 first

Competency Targeting Brief

Assignment Brief

Select a real job description for a role you are genuinely interested in. Decode it into a competency map: identify the top 5 competencies the role demands, list the behavioral indicators for each, and map your 10 stories (from Assignment 2) to those competencies. Identify any gaps and write a one-paragraph "bridge narrative" for each gap.

Format: Competency map + story mapping + bridge narratives 100 pts Self-paced
Module 04 · HR Interview Locked — complete Module 4 first

HR Interview Simulation

Assignment Brief

Record a 10–15 minute solo HR interview simulation. Answer all five of the following questions using your story library and positioning statement: (1) Tell me about yourself. (2) What is your greatest weakness? (3) Why do you want to work here? (4) Tell me about a time you had to deal with a difficult team member. (5) Where do you see yourself in 3 years? Submit the recording and a 200-word self-evaluation.

Format: Recording (10–15 minutes) + 200-word self-evaluation 100 pts Self-paced
Module 05 · Technical & User Locked — complete Module 5 first

Technical Interview Response Portfolio

Assignment Brief

Prepare written responses to five technical/functional questions relevant to your target role and industry. For each response: structure your answer using the problem-decomposition approach from Module 5, think out loud explicitly (write "I would start by...", "My first assumption is..."), and end with a clear synthesis. Each response: 250–400 words.

Format: Five written responses (250–400 words each) 100 pts Self-paced
Module 06 · Final Interview Locked — complete Module 6 first

Final Interview Question Bank

Assignment Brief

Prepare for your final/leadership interview. Write: (1) Your 90-second strategic vision statement (where you are going and why it aligns with this organisation). (2) Responses to three senior-level questions of your choice from the Lesson 6.2 question bank. (3) Five questions you would ask the final interviewer, categorised by type (strategic, operational, relational, future-building). Total: 600–900 words.

Format: Written portfolio (600–900 words) 100 pts Self-paced
Module 07 · Live Simulation Locked — complete Module 7 first

Full Simulation Round — AI Feedback + Peer Debrief

Assignment Brief

Complete a full simulation round in two stages. Stage A — AI Mock Session: run one full AI-powered mock interview session using the tools from Lesson 7.2; submit the AI feedback report (screenshot or export) + your 200-word analysis of the feedback — what the AI identified as your top strengths, your most significant gap, and your specific improvement action. Stage B — Peer Mock Session: conduct a full peer mock interview using the framework from Lesson 7.3 (30–45 minutes); submit the scoring rubric your peer completed for you + a 200-word post-session debrief (what worked, what to improve, one specific technique to implement before the next round).

Format: AI feedback report + 200-word analysis + peer scoring rubric + 200-word debrief 100 pts Self-paced
Module 08 · Offer & Negotiation Locked — complete Module 8 first

Offer Evaluation Worksheet

Assignment Brief

Use a real or realistic fictional offer for a role in your target field. Complete the Offer Evaluation Worksheet: (1) Break down total compensation including all components. (2) Research the market rate for the role (show your sources). (3) Compare the offer to market range. (4) Write your negotiation opening script — the exact words you would say. (5) Write your response to "this is our best offer." Submit as a structured document.

Format: Structured worksheet document 100 pts Self-paced
Module 09 · Probation Success Locked — complete Module 9 first

90-Day Probation Success Plan

Assignment Brief

Design your personal 90-Day Probation Success Plan for your target role. Sections: (1) Day 1–30: Learning priorities and key people to meet (with a stakeholder map). (2) Day 31–60: Your quick win opportunity — what it is, how you will execute it, and how you will communicate it. (3) Day 61–90: How you will prepare for and lead your probation review conversation. Format: structured plan document, 600–900 words, may include tables or frameworks.

Format: Structured plan document (600–900 words) 100 pts Self-paced
