Questions, answered honestly

Can I actually buy a membership today?

Not yet — payment processing is in development, which is why every button on this page starts you on the free tier instead of a checkout. When checkout opens, early-access prices apply and stay locked for as long as you stay subscribed.

Why should I pay when ChatGPT is free?

Use both. ChatGPT will rewrite your CV well. It won't remember what you got wrong last week, know how local ATS parsing differs from the templates it was trained on, tell you your CV is fine and your interview technique is the real problem, or notice you stopped working three weeks ago. We are the system that tells you which tool to use, in what order, and whether it worked.

Is the free tier a crippled trial?

No. The Career Map, your score, and the full Gauntlet are complete products, free forever, no card. The membership adds the pillar curricula and The Compass — it doesn't unlock the things we said were free.

Do you guarantee a job?

No — and we'd encourage you to be careful with anyone who does. We build your readiness; hiring decisions belong to employers. What we do promise: a 14-day refund, honest availability states, and outcome data published truthfully once we have it.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes — a native 3-month split will be available at checkout launch, and we are evaluating financing partners. Career investment shouldn't require having the income the investment is meant to produce.

How do I cancel?

One click, from Settings, effective at the end of your paid period. No retention flows, no phone calls, no countdown timers — ever.
